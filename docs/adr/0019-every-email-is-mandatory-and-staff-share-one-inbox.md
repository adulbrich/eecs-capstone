# Every email is mandatory, and staff share one inbox

There are no notification preferences: a user cannot turn off any email the app sends them, and none of the emails carries an unsubscribe link. Every message is transactional, sent because the recipient must act away from the app (a pickup window, a due date, a request for changes, a suspended account) or because staff owe them the news (a project assigned, a draft deleted); a preference matrix over that set would let someone silence the one message that says their equipment is due, and nobody maintains a matrix. Staff keep the per-action skip on proposer emails, because that is staff deciding to be quiet about one action, not a recipient opting out. Every message addressed to staff goes to one shared mailbox, `EMAIL_STAFF_INBOX`, rather than fanning out to every staff account: fan-out sends the same mail to everyone and teaches them to ignore it, and a shared alias is the thing an instructor already forwards, filters and hands over at the end of a term. Decided 2026-09-11 in #289, shipped in #288.

## Consequences

`EMAIL_REVIEW_INBOX` was renamed `EMAIL_STAFF_INBOX` when it stopped meaning "the review notice"; under the `ses` transport the app refuses to boot without it, the way it refuses without `EMAIL_FROM`, because a staff-facing email with nowhere to go is a queue that fills up unwatched. No `notification_preferences` table exists and no settings page offers one; a request for either reopens this decision rather than adding a toggle. A borrower with an address and no account receives the pickup and due emails at that address, since email is the only channel that can reach them and there is no preference that could say otherwise.
