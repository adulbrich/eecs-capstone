# Every email is mandatory, and staff share one inbox

There are no notification preferences and no unsubscribe link: every email is transactional, sent because the recipient must act away from the app or because staff owe them the news, and a preference matrix would let someone silence the one message saying their equipment is due. Staff may skip the email their own action would send; the skip is named before the click, defaults to sending, and is ignored from a non-staff actor. Every message to staff goes to one shared mailbox, `EMAIL_STAFF_INBOX`, rather than to each staff account, because fan-out teaches everyone to ignore it and a shared alias is what an instructor already forwards and hands over; under the `ses` transport the app refuses to boot without it. A holder with an address and no account receives pickup and due mail at that address. A request for a preferences table reopens this decision. Decided 2026-09-11 in #289.
