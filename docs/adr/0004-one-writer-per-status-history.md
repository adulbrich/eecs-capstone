# `transitionItem` is the only inventory writer; `commitTransition` the only project status writer

`transitionItem` in `src/server/_internal/inventory-transitions.ts` is the only writer of `inventory_item_status_history` and the only thing that syncs the `current_holder_*` columns with an item's status; approve, reject, cancel and submit all route through it, passing what is theirs as `authority` and `lineDecision`, because an earlier exemption for reject and cancel left two of four writers ignorant of two new hold columns. Projects make the narrower claim on purpose: `commitTransition` in `src/server/_internal/projects.ts` is the only writer of `project_status_history` and owns the ordering, notifications inside the transaction and the AI refresh and the email strictly after commit, so a failed send cannot undo an approval. `projects.status` has other legitimate writers, and soft delete and restore are not transitions. Both counts are checked by grep rather than by file name, and the dev seed drives the real write path, because a second writer that disagreed produced holds with no deadlines, lines, history or notifications.
