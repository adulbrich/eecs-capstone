# A missing role is refused in place

A signed-in viewer who opens a page their role does not allow gets an access-denied page at that URL, answered 403 on a server render, naming the account they are signed in with and the role the page needs; they are not redirected. The staff routes used to send such a viewer to `/` (or an instructor on `/admin/users` to `/admin`) without a word, which read as a broken link and left nothing in the address bar to report. A 404 was the other option and was rejected: the repository is public, so hiding that `/admin` exists buys nothing, and a 404 would tell somebody signed in with the wrong address that the page is gone rather than that they need another account. The guards are `requireStaff` and `requireAdmin` in `src/lib/access-denied.ts`, which throw a plain object rather than an `Error` because a server render dehydrates an `Error` down to its message; the router's `defaultErrorComponent` renders the page, and `src/server.ts` turns router-core's 500 into the 403. Out of scope, and unchanged: a record a role can see but a rule hides (another user's draft project), and the project edit page, which still sends a viewer who cannot edit back to the project. Decided 2026-09-24 in #606.
