# A missing role is refused in place

A signed-in viewer who opens a page their role does not allow gets an access-denied page at that URL, answered 403 on a server render, naming the account they are signed in with and the role the page needs, rather than a redirect. The staff routes used to send them to `/` without a word, which read as a broken link. A 404 was rejected: the repository is public, so hiding `/admin` buys nothing, and a 404 tells someone signed in with the wrong address that the page is gone rather than that they need another account. `requireStaff` and `requireAdmin` in `src/lib/access-denied.ts` throw a plain object, because a server render dehydrates an `Error` to its message; the router's `defaultErrorComponent` renders the page and `src/server.ts` turns the 500 into the 403. A record a rule hides, such as another user's draft, and the project edit page keep their behaviour. Decided 2026-09-24 in #606.
