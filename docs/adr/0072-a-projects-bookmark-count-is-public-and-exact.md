# A project's bookmark count is public and exact

A published project's page shows its bookmark count to everyone, signed in or not, as the last badge under its title: "0 bookmarks", "1 bookmark", "12 bookmarks" (#906). It counts what the staff tables on `/admin/analytics` count, bookmarks since publication on a published project (#518), through the same query, so the page and the table never disagree; any other status shows no badge. Who bookmarked stays private to that user. The count may herd students toward one-team projects that are already crowded, against #518's aim of spreading demand. Not taken: the proposer first and students after a placement cycle, demand against teams, nothing below a threshold or a "fewer than 4" band, hiding it during bidding (the app has no bidding dates), the count on the card and the tables, a sort by it, and measuring the herding against first-choice bids. On merge, 32 published projects read "0 bookmarks" in public, and the count never hides. Revisit if staff see first choices pile onto the most-bookmarked projects. Decided 2026-11-21.
