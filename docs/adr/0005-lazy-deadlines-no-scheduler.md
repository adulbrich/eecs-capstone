# Deadlines are informational and overdue is derived; there is no scheduler

Pickup and due dates on request lines and items are columns nothing acts on. Overdue is derived from them and the clock by `src/lib/inventory-deadlines.ts` whenever something asks, so a page and the notification scan cannot disagree about it, and overdue notifications are written lazily on read, deduplicated on `(user_id, type, link)` by a partial unique index; the app runs with no cron, no queue and no second process. The cost is that a notice arrives when someone next opens a page that scans, not when the deadline passes, and a hold on an address with no account cannot be notified until staff link it. The analytics dashboard's overdue count is the one sanctioned SQL twin of the rule, pinned by a test that seeds one of each case.
