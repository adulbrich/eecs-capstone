# `sourcing` rather than `approved` on a custom line, and no `returned`

A custom line has the same five slots as a request line, undecided, decided yes and still live, succeeded, refused, withdrawn, with two words swapped on purpose. The live-yes state is `sourcing`, not `approved`, because approving a request line hands over a physical thing: the item becomes reserved to that person with a pickup deadline. A custom line has no item, so the same word would name a promise on one screen and a reservation on the next, in a queue that shows both kinds side by side. The success state is `fulfilled`, not `returned`, because the two kinds succeed at different things: a borrow succeeds when the thing comes back, an acquisition when the thing exists. A custom line never lends anything; borrowing the item that was bought is an ordinary request line, with a `returned` of its own. One deliberate divergence follows from `sourcing` meaning "we are getting it": a custom line may be rejected from `sourcing`, where a request line can only be rejected while `pending`, because an order can fall through and the alternative is a line stuck sourcing with no way out but a lie.

## Consequences

`INVENTORY_CUSTOM_LINE_STATUSES` is its own tuple in `src/lib/vocabularies.ts` with its own `pgEnum`, not a widening of the request line's, and the queue's status filter is the union of the two built from the tuples; a status only one kind has filters to that kind with no special case. `CONTEXT.md`'s Line status entry, which listed `fulfilled` as a word to avoid, now says "for a request line": it is the custom line's own status, and leaving the glossary contradicting itself would break its one-definition-per-term rule. Two speaking transitions get two note columns, `sourcing_note` and `outcome_note`, so fulfilling never overwrites what sourcing said, and neither is the sibling's `review_comment`, which the requester's projection deliberately hides; a name that means two things across one queue is the hazard this decision exists to avoid. The `open | closed | all` filter on `/my/items` counts `sourcing` as open and `fulfilled` as closed, and keeps a fulfilled line visible beside the open hold it produced.
