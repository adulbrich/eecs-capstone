# The status vocabularies live in src/lib, not in the schema

Each status vocabulary is one `as const` tuple in `src/lib/vocabularies.ts`: `src/db/schema.ts` passes it to `pgEnum`, and the client-safe modules derive their unions with `(typeof T)[number]`, because copies in the schema, a `src/lib` union and every filtering route had drifted with nothing linking them. The direction is the decision. Deriving from `enumValues` would be less code but pulls `src/db/schema.ts`, and `drizzle-orm` with it, into every client bundle that names a status, while tuples in `src/lib` import nothing. `src/lib/__tests__/vocabulary-scan.ts` fails on a whole vocabulary written out anywhere else in `src/`, and it scans every tuple in that file, which is why `USER_ROLES` lives there too (ADR-0016). A deliberately narrower union such as `RequestLineOutcome` is held to its column by a `Part extends Whole` constraint, and the one reader's order, `PROJECT_STATUSES_IN_DISPLAY_ORDER`, is a `Record` keyed by the union so an unranked status fails to compile.
