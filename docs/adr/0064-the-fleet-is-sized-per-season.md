# The fleet is sized per season

Off season the app runs two tasks with a ceiling of two, a pool of 40 on `db.t4g.micro` ((40 + 5) * 2 + 10 = 100 of 107), and Container Insights off; at term start it runs the term values of ADR-0034 (a pool of 45), ADR-0035 (`db.t4g.small`) and ADR-0040 (three to four tasks), and ADR-0043's deploy percentages hold in both. After the 2026 term start's first week traffic fell from 308,195 requests a day to under 5,000 from 10-13, and RDS CPU stayed at or under 7.1% outside a load test and one five-minute spike. Depth beats a third task off season because a staff load of `/admin/analytics` opens up to 30 connections on one task, where the student peak opened 8. A floor of one was rejected: a deploy then keeps no task. Container Insights' metrics were about $11 of a $97 month, and the fleet alarm reads the free `HealthyHostCount` instead (ADR-0058); at two tasks it fires only at none, so one task down is left to the 5XX alarms. The month falls to about $62. `db.t4g.micro` sat at 131 to 180 MiB freeable in September, so `eecs-capstone-db-memory` alarms below 64 MiB, and the answer to it is the larger class. `db-pool.test.ts` holds `rdsUsable` to the instance class. #793 restores the term values in late August, Terraform before the deploy. Decided 2026-10-19 in #527.
