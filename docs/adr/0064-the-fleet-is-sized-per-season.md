# The fleet is sized per season

Off season the app runs two tasks with a ceiling of two, a pool of 40 on `db.t4g.micro` ((40 + 5) * 2 + 10 = 100 of 107), and Container Insights off; at term start it runs ADR-0034, ADR-0035 and ADR-0040's three to four tasks, a pool of 45 and `db.t4g.small`. The 2026 term start never needed the term sizing after its first week: traffic fell from 308,195 requests a day to under 5,000, and RDS CPU stayed under 7.2% outside one five-minute spike. Depth beats a third task off season because a staff load of `/admin/analytics` opens up to 30 connections on one task, where the student peak opened 8. A floor of one was rejected because a deploy at 50 and 100 percent then has no task to keep. Container Insights' metrics were about $11 of a $97 month, and the fleet alarm reads the free `HealthyHostCount` instead (ADR-0058). The month falls to about $62. `db.t4g.micro` sat at 131 to 180 MiB freeable in September, so `eecs-capstone-db-memory` alarms below 64 MiB, and the answer to it is the larger class. `db-pool.test.ts` holds `rdsUsable` to the instance class. #793 restores the term values in late August, Terraform before the deploy. Decided 2026-10-19 in #527.
