# Teams supported has two writers

`projects.teams_supported` has two writers: the proposer on the project form, and staff in the staff panel's Programs and teams section. ADR-0026 rejected that shape for the program and the openings flag, and the exception is deliberate: those are staff judgements a proposer cannot make, while teams supported is capacity a proposer states about themselves and staff cannot know. Taking it from the proposer would leave it with the party that knows less, and leaving it with them alone meant staff placing a project in a second program had to email to raise the count (#468). `updateProjectProgramsAs` writes it under the section's one Save with the form's 1 to 5 bounds, and the panel says the proposer owns the field; neither writer is told the other moved it, which is #109's. The count is a public badge in `ProjectBadges`, silent at one. Decided 2026-09-18 in #468.
