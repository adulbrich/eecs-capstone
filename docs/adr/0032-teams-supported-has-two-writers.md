# Teams supported has two writers

`projects.teams_supported` is written by the proposer, on `/projects/new` and `/projects/$projectId/edit`, and by staff, from the Programs and teams section of the staff panel. It is the only field in the project domain with two writers in two places, and the exception is deliberate. [ADR-0026](./0026-the-program-is-staff-placed-not-proposed.md) rejected exactly this shape for the program and then for the openings flag, on the grounds that one value with two writers is how the proposer and staff come to disagree about it with nobody told (#109). Both of those fields are staff judgements a proposer cannot make: which course a project runs in depends on how a term is being filled, and whether a team filled up is something staff learn from running bidding and assignment. How many teams a proposer can host is the opposite. It is capacity they state about themselves, from their own supervision budget and scope, and staff have no way to know it, so taking the field from them the way #491 took the openings flag would leave the number set by the party with less information. Against that, `TeamsWarning` tells staff a project runs in more programs than its count allows, and before this change the sentence ended by telling them to email the proposer, which is the friction #468 exists to remove: staff placing a project in a second program need to raise the count in the same motion. Decided 2026-09-18 in #468.

## Consequences

`programsSchema` carries `teamsSupported` beside `programIds` and `acceptingApplicants`, with the same 1 to 5 bounds `projectInputSchema` enforces, and `updateProjectProgramsAs` writes it under the section's one Save; the proposer's form is unchanged. The panel's number input carries a note saying the proposer owns the field and can set it back, which is the whole mitigation: neither writer is told the other moved it, and closing that gap is #109's, not this one's. `TeamsWarning` keeps its predicate and now points at the input above it rather than at somebody to email. The count left the Private panel's header, where a comment had called it proposer-and-staff information that therefore belonged outside the staff panel, and became a public badge in `ProjectBadges`, silent at one team; it was already public in the Teams supported column of `/projects` table view and `/my/bookmarks`, which is why making it visible on the card and the detail page is not a disclosure. The two tables do not pass the badge a count, because they already print it in a column of their own. Per-program counts would remove the program-versus-teams mismatch rather than making it easier to fix; that is #469 and this decision does not stand in its way.
