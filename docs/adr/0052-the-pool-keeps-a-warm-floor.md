# The pool keeps a warm floor of five connections

In production the app pool keeps `POOL_MIN`, five connections per task, open through any lull, and `warmPool` opens them on the task's first request, the health check. A load test drew 500s from tasks whose pool had nothing open when a burst arrived: pg-pool closes a client idle for 10 s, and opening every connection on a saturated 0.25 vCPU overran the 5 s `connectionTimeoutMillis` (#601). `min` alone opens nothing and a warm-up alone is closed ten seconds later, so both are needed. The warm-up runs from `src/server.ts` rather than a Nitro plugin, which is bundled apart and would warm a pool no route queries. The floor sits inside `POOL_MAX`, so ADR-0034's budget holds. Dev, tests and scripts keep no floor, and a connection lost to a failover is not reopened until a deploy or a burst of five. Five is the issue's number, not a measurement. Decided 2026-09-23.
