# A comment is edited in place, and the reply is what freezes it

An author may rewrite their own comment's text, and the row keeps no copy of what it said before: `project_comments` gains `edited_at` and nothing else, so the page can say the words moved without being able to say what they were. That sits against [ADR-0008](./0008-account-deletion-anonymizes.md), which names comments among the audit records a `user` row exists to anchor and refuses to hard delete an account because of it, so the shift deserves saying out loud. What makes it safe is the lock rather than the history: once anything has replied to a comment, by anyone, internal or not, the comment is frozen, and a reply is what a review conversation leaves behind when it has used a comment for something. A comment nobody has answered is still a draft in every sense but the storage. Two other things stand in for the version history a reader might reach for: the `(edited)` marker with its time, which tells staff reading their own internal thread that the parent moved under them, and the email, which went out with the original words the moment the comment was posted ([ADR-0019](./0019-every-email-is-mandatory-and-staff-share-one-inbox.md)) and cannot be recalled. Keeping every version would mean a table, a projection, a retention answer and a place to read it, for a feature whose case is a typo. Decided 2026-09-18 in #503; deleting a comment is a separate question, open in #514.

## Consequences

`updateCommentAs` is the only writer, and it checks membership and then authorship: the current proposer or staff may be in the thread, and only the person who wrote a comment may rewrite it, staff included. Membership is not redundant with authorship, because staff reassign a project's proposer and the previous one keeps the comments they wrote while leaving the conversation they belong to. The reply lock rides on the `UPDATE` as a `NOT EXISTS`, not on a read before it, so a reply landing mid-edit stops the edit rather than racing it. Nothing about an edit notifies: no email, no `notifications` row, and no rewrite of the 200 character snapshot `commentNotifications` stored when the comment was posted, which leaves the bell agreeing with the inbox rather than with the database. `is_internal` is not editable at all, since flipping it would either pretend to retract text already sent or expose an aside whose replies inherited their flag at write time. The lock reaches the client as `hasReply`, computed over every reply on the project before `filterCommentsForViewer` runs, because a proposer is handed no children for a comment staff replied to internally; `docs/QUIRKS.md` carries the ordering.
