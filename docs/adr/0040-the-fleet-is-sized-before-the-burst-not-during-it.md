# The fleet is sized before the burst, not during it

`app_min_tasks` is 3 and `app_max_tasks` stays 4, with the target tracking policy unchanged, because scaling out is slower than a term start arrival: the #524 load test measured new tasks serving about six minutes after the third breaching minute, against a burst lasting about two. Two tasks absorbed about 26 requests per second against the roughly 42 a 500 student start needs, and three reach about 39 at saturation, the cheapest floor in reach that still leaves the scaler a fourth. Scheduled scaling to four for the first week of term was rejected as a moving part whose failure is silently not firing on the one morning it exists for. The accepted gap is a brief saturation at each term start, which only a floor of four closes. Decided 2026-09-21.
