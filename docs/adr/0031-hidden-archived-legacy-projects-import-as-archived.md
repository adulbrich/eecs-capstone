# The legacy projects the old portal hid are imported as `archived`, and so are public here

The legacy portal has two independent flags, `cp_archived` and `cp_is_hidden`, and 146 of the 714 retired Accepting Applicants rows it held on 2026-09-17 carried both. On an archived row the second one is inert there: `getBrowsableCapstoneProjects` requires `cp_archived = false`, so a retired project is off the Browse page whether or not it is hidden. What the flag records is the state the project was in when it was retired, because publishing is the only thing that clears it and the column defaults to set. The log bears that out with no exceptions: every one of the 557 rows that is not hidden has a `Project Published` entry, and among the hidden ones 81 have one too, 11 created after the log began in 2022-08 verifiably never did, and 54 predate the log and cannot be told apart. They were held out of the 2026-09-16 import on the ground that this app's archive is public, so importing them would publish what the old portal had taken off its shelf, and they were imported on 2026-09-18 once that trade-off was accepted. `archived` is the only status that fits. `approved` would be a trap rather than a compromise: `TRANSITIONS` has no `approved -> archived` edge, so 146 retired projects would sit in a status whose only forward move is to publish them, and `approved` claims they are awaiting publication when they are finished. Soft-deleting them on import would work semantically but `deleted_at` is not in the importer's upsert. Decided 2026-09-18.

## Consequences

145 projects that the old portal had unlisted are now reachable here by link and through the archived filter. 80 of them are merely public again rather than public for the first time; the other 65 are what this decision actually exposes, 11 of which verifiably never went public and 54 of which predate the log. Staff can archive-and-soft-delete any that should not be there. The 146th row was unarchived and published in the old portal on 2026-09-17, and it is one of the 81 with a publish entry, which is why the split above is 80 and 65 rather than 81 and 64. `archived` is in `EMBEDDABLE_STATUSES`, so unlike the 64 live `approved` rows these cost a Bedrock call each: the backfill that followed updated 146 vectors. `clean-export.py` no longer writes a hidden side file, because nothing is held back any more, and the only rows it still sets aside are the DigiClips working notes. What stays in the old portal is drafts, rejections and archived submissions nobody ruled on, none of which has a home in this app's status vocabulary.
