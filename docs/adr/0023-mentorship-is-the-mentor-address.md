# Mentorship is the mentor address, and nothing else

A project's mentorship is `mentor_email`, one nullable column, staff-written and never public. It replaced the three-state `mentor_need` enum of [ADR-0022](./0022-mentorship-is-a-three-state-column-that-excludes-an-address.md) and the two flags it derived, because most of what the state served was would-be mentors shopping for a team on the public listing, which is not a feature this app wants, and a student-facing "no formal mentor" notice, which is not needed either: the description can say it. What staff actually need is to record who mentors a project, see which student-proposed projects have nobody yet, and match those against the volunteers on `/admin/mentors`, and a null address answers all three. No third state, no "seeking" mark, no refusal: a mentor on a project that did not strictly need one is a fact, not a contradiction, which is the non-technical partner case, and an instructor who runs a team without an outside mentor records their own address. The alternative, keeping a staff-only state with no public face, was withdrawn on the same day: it kept a column, a radio group and a refusal for a distinction the null already draws. Decided 2026-09-14 in #402.

## Consequences

`mentor_need`, its enum type, `MENTOR_NEEDS`, `src/lib/mentor-need.ts`, the "Seeking mentor" and "No mentor needed" badges and the two public switches are gone; migration `0028` drops the column and the type with no backfill, since nothing is in production. The public listing offers four switches. `/admin/projects` gains an admin-only "Without a mentor" switch (`mentor_email IS NULL`), which beside "Student proposed" is the staff to-do, and the dashboard tile counts the same two columns. `projectSummarySelect` and `projectDetailView` carry no key about the mentor, and the key-set pins say so. Volunteering (the profile toggle, the team count, `/admin/mentors`, the capacity tiles) is untouched.
