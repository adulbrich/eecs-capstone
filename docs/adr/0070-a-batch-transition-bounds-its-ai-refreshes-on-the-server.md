# A batch transition bounds its AI refreshes on the server

Staff archive every published project, or publish every approved one, in the admin list's view in one confirm (#889), and each project then owes the embedding and social summary refresh a single transition starts. The batch starts them after its one commit, at most `BATCH_REFRESH_CONCURRENCY` (4) at a time on the server, each on its own project's queue. Not taken: the categorization backfill's browser runner, one project per call with a stop button, after placement in the browser (ADR-0056). A runner fits where the slow work is the action; here the action is the status change and the refresh is its side effect (ADR-0053). A per-project transition would lose the all-or-nothing batch, flip the catalog over minutes and need the tab open; refreshes alone in the browser would leave projects published with no embedding and no record of a missed try once the tab closed. Not taken either: every refresh at once, about 80 Bedrock calls for 40 projects, which could throttle the staff panels' own. The dialog shows no refresh progress. A task recycled mid-batch drops the queued refreshes, as for any save, and the two backfill scripts recover them. Decided 2026-11-17.
