# The task-count alarm sits below what a deploy keeps running

`eecs-capstone-fleet-below-floor` in `infra/alarms.tf` alarms when the target group's `HealthyHostCount` is below `ceil(app_min_tasks * deployment_minimum_healthy_percent / 100)` for three one-minute periods: below two while the floor is three, below one at the off-season floor of two. Replaces ADR-0044, which alarmed at the floor and so mailed on 12 of 12 deploys and on nothing else; no sample count separates a deploy's seven to ten minute dip from a real loss, so the threshold is the dip itself, read from the service so the two move together. It read Container Insights' `RunningTaskCount` until ADR-0064 turned Container Insights off; the health check count also catches a task that runs but hangs. What it gives up, a fleet short by what a deploy stops, is answered by ECS replacement, the deployment circuit breaker and the 5XX alarms. It holds only while the healthy percent is above 0. `treat_missing_data` stays `breaching`, because a target group with nothing registered may publish nothing. Decided 2026-09-30 in #727.
