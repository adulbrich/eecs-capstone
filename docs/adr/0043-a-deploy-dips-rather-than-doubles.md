# A deploy dips rather than doubles, so the pool can be deep

`infra/ecs.tf` sets `deployment_maximum_percent` to 100 and `deployment_minimum_healthy_percent` to 50, so a deploy stops a task before starting its replacement and the task ceiling is `app_max_tasks` rather than twice it. That is what lets the pool be deep: the #524 load test found the pool, not CPU, failing under a term start burst (#558), and `(45 + 5) * 4 + 10 = 210` fits the instance's 220. The trade is deploy-time capacity, a fleet of three dipping to two for about three minutes per task, accepted because a deploy is an operator's choice while the pool ceiling bound every burst. `deregistration_delay` is 60 seconds so three sequential replacements fit the workflow's wait, and availability zone rebalancing is off because ECS refuses 100 percent with it on. Do not deploy during the first hour of a term start morning or during any announced arrival, and apply Terraform before the deploy that raises the pool. A bigger instance was rejected because RDS CPU peaked at 13%. Decided 2026-09-21.
